# Mobile App (Field Technician Guide)

The FieldForce Pro mobile app is available for iOS and Android. It gives field technicians everything they need without needing a laptop.

## Logging In

1. Open the FieldForce Pro app.
2. Enter your work email and password.
3. Tap **Sign In**.
4. If biometric login is configured, you can use Face ID or Fingerprint after the first login.

## Home Screen (Tabs)

| Tab | Contents |
|-----|----------|
| **Home** | Today's summary: upcoming jobs, notifications |
| **Schedule** | Calendar view of assigned appointments |
| **Map** | Live navigation to job sites |
| **More** | Documents, service locations, parts catalogue, settings, logout |

## Viewing Assigned Work Orders

1. Tap the **Schedule** tab.
2. Today's appointments are shown at the top.
3. Tap an appointment to open the linked work order.

## Updating Work Order Status

1. Open the work order from the Schedule or a notification.
2. Tap the **Status** button (e.g., `Scheduled`).
3. Select the next status (e.g., `In Progress`).
4. Confirm. The dispatcher sees the update in real time on the web dashboard.

## Navigating to a Job Site

1. Open an appointment or work order.
2. Tap **Navigate** — this opens the device's native maps app (Google Maps or Apple Maps) with turn-by-turn directions to the service location.

## Clocking In and Out (Timesheets)

1. Open a work order you are assigned to.
2. Tap **Clock In** to start the timesheet entry.
3. Tap **Clock Out** when the job is complete.
4. The time entry is recorded and visible to your manager.
   - Alternatively, access **More → Timesheets** to manually add a time entry.

## Adding Notes to a Work Order

1. Open the work order.
2. Scroll to the **Notes** section.
3. Tap **+ Add Note**, type your note, and tap **Submit**.

## Uploading Photos

1. Open the work order.
2. Tap the **Attachments** section.
3. Tap **Take Photo** to use the camera, or **Upload from Library** to select an existing image.
4. Add an optional caption.
5. Tap **Upload**.

## Completing a Job Sheet / Checklist

1. Open the work order.
2. Tap the **Job Sheet** tab.
3. Work through each checklist item — tap to mark complete, add values, or capture signatures.
4. Tap **Submit Job Sheet** when all items are completed.

## Capturing a Customer Signature

1. On the work order or estimate detail screen, tap **Capture Signature**.
2. Hand the device to the customer.
3. They sign on the screen with their finger.
4. Tap **Accept** to save the signature.

## Offline Mode

The mobile app works without internet. When offline:
- Previously loaded work orders and contacts are accessible.
- Notes, status updates, and photos are queued locally.
- When connectivity is restored, all changes sync automatically.
- A yellow banner at the top indicates offline status.

## Push Notifications

Technicians receive push notifications for:
- New work order assignment
- Appointment time changes
- Dispatcher messages
- New notes added by the office

To enable: ensure notifications are allowed for the FieldForce Pro app in your device settings.

## Logging Out

1. Tap the **More** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom.
3. Tap **Logout** and confirm.
