# Voice Notes and AI Vision Features

---

## Voice Notes

### What are Voice Notes?
Voice notes let technicians record spoken audio on the mobile app while at a job site. Instead of typing notes with gloves on or while holding tools, the technician can quickly dictate what they observed, what they repaired, or what the customer said.

The voice note is automatically transcribed to text and attached to the work order.

### Accessing Voice Notes
- On the **mobile app**: Open a work order → scroll to the **Notes** section → tap the **Microphone** icon.
- On the **web app**: Go to **Voice Notes** in the sidebar to see all recorded voice notes.

### Recording a Voice Note (Mobile App)
1. Open the work order on the mobile app.
2. Tap the **Microphone** icon in the Notes section.
3. Tap **Record** and speak clearly.
4. Tap **Stop** when finished.
5. The system transcribes the audio to text automatically.
6. Review the transcription (edit if needed) and tap **Save**.
7. The note is added to the work order's Notes tab with a "Voice" label.

### Viewing Voice Notes (Web)
1. Go to **Voice Notes** in the sidebar.
2. The list shows all recorded voice notes with the transcription preview, work order, technician, and timestamp.
3. Click a voice note to see the full transcription and play the original audio recording.

### Voice Note Tips
- Speak clearly and at a normal pace for best transcription accuracy.
- Mention key details: what the fault was, what parts were used, what was done to fix it.
- Voice notes are transcribed in the language the technician speaks (multi-language support).
- Transcription is usually complete within 10–30 seconds.

## AI Vision (Document Intelligence and Photo Analysis)

### What is AI Vision?
AI Vision uses artificial intelligence to extract information from photos and documents automatically. Instead of manually typing details from photos or scanned documents, the AI reads them for you.

### Use Cases for AI Vision

#### 1. Invoice / Document OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
Upload a scanned invoice, receipt, or document and the AI extracts:
- Supplier name
- Invoice number
- Line items (description, quantity, unit price)
- Total amounts
- Due date

This saves time when processing supplier invoices or expense documents.

#### 2. Asset Photo Analysis
When a technician uploads a photo of a piece of equipment, the AI can:
- Identify the asset model from the photo
- Read the serial number or model plate
- Detect visible damage or fault indicators
- Suggest likely fault codes based on what is visible

#### 3. Job Site Photo Tagging
Photos uploaded to a work order can be automatically tagged with:
- "Before" or "After" the repair
- Identified objects (e.g., "electrical panel", "pipe leak", "AC unit")
- Location context (inside, outside, roof, basement)

### Accessing AI Vision
Go to **Vision** in the sidebar (or **AI → Vision Dashboard**).

### Using AI Vision on a Document
1. Go to **Vision** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Upload Document** or drag and drop a file.
3. The AI processes the document and extracts the data.
4. Review the extracted fields.
5. Click **Apply to Invoice** to create an invoice from the extracted data, or **Apply to Work Order** to fill in work order fields.

### Using AI Vision from a Work Order
1. Open the work order and go to the **Attachments** tab.
2. Upload a photo or document.
3. Click **Analyse with AI** on the uploaded file.
4. The AI returns extracted information that can be applied to the work order.

### Vision Dashboard
The **Vision Dashboard** shows:
- Recently processed documents and their extracted data
- Processing success rate
- Documents pending review
- Documents where extraction confidence was low (needs manual review)
