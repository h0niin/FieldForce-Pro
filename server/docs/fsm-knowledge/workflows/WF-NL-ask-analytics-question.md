# WF-NL-ask-analytics-question — Ask a Data Question in Natural Language

## Prerequisites
- User must have the `analytics:read` permission.

## Steps

1. Click **Analytics** in the left sidebar.
2. The Natural Language Analytics page opens with a large search/question input box.
3. Type your question in plain English in the input box. Examples:
   - "Show me all work orders completed last month by technician"
   - "What was our total revenue in Q3?"
   - "Which customers had the most overdue invoices?"
   - "How many jobs breached SLA in the past 30 days?"
   - "Compare technician performance this month vs last month"
4. Press **Enter** or click the **Ask** button (usually an arrow icon or magnifying glass).
5. The system processes your question (usually takes 2–5 seconds).
6. A chart and/or data table appears with the answer.
7. To refine your results, type a follow-up question. For example, after seeing revenue data, type "Now break it down by territory".
8. To export the result, click **Export CSV** or **Download Chart** if those options appear.
9. To save the result as a report, click **Save as Report** and give it a name.

## Tips for Better Questions
- Be specific about the time period: "last month", "Q2 2025", "past 7 days", "year to date"
- Mention the entity: "work orders", "invoices", "technicians", "customers", "assets"
- Use comparisons for trend questions: "compare this week with last week"

## Result
A visual chart or data table is shown answering your question. The answer can be saved as a reusable report.
